# Incomplete examples

Two Table 2 cases are kept here in a compact form rather than being expanded into complete decision records.

`PG-ST1` is resolved during evidential screening before a `B` state is constructed, and the case description does not specify the stakeholder attribution needed for a full reason record. `PG-ST5` supplies the persistent disagreement and jurisdictional warrant but leaves the two standing-qualified reason sets implicit.

The compact files retain the fields needed to identify the case and its expected disposition without supplying additional stakeholder or reason relationships.
