## Protocol-property checks

### Prevalence invariance

Describe the controlled multiplicity change and record whether the Gate regime remained stable.

### Evidence responsiveness

Describe the evidential change and record whether the affected proposition remained in `B_Π(d)`.

### Standing transparency

Record each inclusion or exclusion that changed `B_Π(d)` and identify the declared standing, evidential, or materiality basis for that change.

### Resolution burden

For each `RESOLVE` outcome under a nonempty conflict set, record the sufficient resolution warrant and the material unresolved conflicts it covers.

## Counterfactual tests

Record results for any applicable `PG-CF1`–`PG-CF6` perturbations.

## Policy context

Record the standing profile and warrant policy used in the evaluation. When several policy profiles are evaluated, report results separately for each profile.
