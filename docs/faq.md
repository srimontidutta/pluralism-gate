# Frequently Asked Questions

## Does the Gate determine which contested position is morally correct?

The Gate evaluates decision authority under surviving standing-grounded conflict. It determines whether the declared deployment policy permits unilateral commitment, consultation, representation, or deferral for the decision under review. The legitimacy and representativeness of the standing and warrant policies remain subjects for governance analysis.

## Can a majority of retrieved documents create decision authority?

Retrieved prevalence is kept separate from standing and authority throughout the framework. Repetition or numerical dominance in retrieved material therefore cannot supply a resolution warrant.

## Which disagreements reach the Gate?

The applicable evidential or uncertainty procedure handles purely empirical disagreement. The Gate addresses residual normative or action-justificatory disagreement that remains after domain-appropriate evidential screening.

## What does a B state record?

A `B` state records material admissible reasons on both sides of an action-relevant proposition after the evidential, standing, and materiality filters have been applied. The representation records the coexistence of support and opposition without assigning equal probability, prevalence, or normative weight to either side.

## When can a legal rule function as a resolution warrant?

A binding jurisdictional rule can supply a warrant when the declared policy treats it as controlling among otherwise feasible contested alternatives. Rules that determine feasibility or independent permissibility remain part of the surrounding task policy.

## When can a user preference function as a warrant?

In a user-controlled domain, the declared policy may recognize the user's stated preference as sufficient authority for the contested commitment. The paper's personal travel-planning case uses this structure.

## How is an advisory task handled?

When material conflict remains and the current task calls for presentation rather than commitment, the Gate returns `REPRESENT`.

## How is required commitment handled when a warrant is absent?

A policy-valid consultation path leads to `CONSULT` when it can materially supply or clarify the missing authority before action. When such a path is unavailable, the Gate returns `DEFER`.

## How can a benchmark represent contested standing?

The paper allows several declared standing profiles to be retained for the same scenario. Evaluations can then report the permitted regime under each profile instead of collapsing the policy disagreement into one gold label.

## Can the audit record accompany a full agent trace?

Yes. The compact decision record can sit within a richer trace that retains additional system events, while preserving the authority-related information needed by the framework.
